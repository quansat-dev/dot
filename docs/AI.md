# About the Use of GenAI within This Project

In this text, the term "Generative AI (genAI)" refers to contemporary transformer-based models, their harnesses, and technologies with similar characteristics that are used to generate content, such as text, images, audio, and video.

## Is GenAI Allowed here?

You may use GenAI when working on this project. I ask, however, that you be mindful about its limitation and biases. Please:

- review carefully all generated content,
- document and disclose with as much detail as possible when you use GenAI in issues, pull requests, and discussions.

I also ask, even though it may seem to be difficult or antiquated, that you think twice before using GenAI.

## Where GenAI may be helpful

- suggest pointers and directions when researching new topic or areas of knowledge that you are not familiar with.
- review code for local (same file, within PR) consistency and security.
